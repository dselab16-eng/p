ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

A university built around frontier technology.

The University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB) is a specialized public university located in Kaliakair, Gazipur. The university was established through an Act of Parliament in 2016, with academic activities beginning in 2019.

UFTB's academic and research direction is strongly connected to information technology, engineering, emerging technologies, innovation and the needs of the Fourth and Fifth Industrial Revolutions.

RESEARCH & INNOVATION

Research at the frontier.

UFTB's research portfolio spans artificial intelligence, data science, cybersecurity, IoT, robotics, education technology and interdisciplinary applications.

Explainable AI

Research into interpretable AI systems, including student performance prediction and explainable machine learning.

Bangla NLP

Deep learning approaches for Bangla text normalization, emotion classification and expressive text-to-speech.

AI in Education

Intelligent learning systems, personalized learning and AI-assisted education.

Predictive Analytics

Data-driven models for forecasting, decision-making and real-world applications.

Big Data

Research involving large-scale datasets, data mining and computational analysis.

Bioinformatics

Machine learning and computational analysis applied to biomedical research.

Intrusion Detection

Intelligent security frameworks for detecting threats in software-defined networks.

Secure Systems

Security engineering, privacy and dependable computing systems.

Network Security

Research focused on securing modern communication infrastructure.

Robotics

Autonomous systems, robotics algorithms and real-world engineering applications.

IoT Systems

Sensor networks, embedded systems and connected intelligent devices.

Smart Applications

IoT-driven applications integrating sensors, mobile platforms and machine learning.

MESSAGE FROM THE VICE-CHANCELLOR

A future driven by knowledge, technology and innovation.
